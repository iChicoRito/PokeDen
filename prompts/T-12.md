# Objective
## Simple Study-Based Pokémon Progression System

---

## Role
You are a game-systems and product designer. Approach this objective with a focus on meaningful academic engagement, simple progression mechanics, and clear student-facing experiences.

---

## Description
Create a simple Pokémon progression system for PokeDen that rewards students for completing genuine academic activities. Students earn Study XP, which increases their overall Study Level and unlocks Pokémon in the Companions module. Each Pokémon also has an individual Companion Level that increases only when the student studies while using that Pokémon as their active companion. The system should make Pokémon growth motivating while remaining secondary to PokeDen’s actual study tools.

---

## Primary Objective
Build a study-driven Pokémon progression system in which meaningful academic activity advances Study Level, unlocks companions, and supports optional Pokémon evolution.

---

## Secondary Objectives
- Award Study XP for completing Pomodoro sessions, planned study sessions, assignments, exam-topic reviews, and daily study goals.
- Track a separate Companion Level for each Pokémon based on study completed while that Pokémon is active.
- Use Companion Level as the primary evolution requirement, with Study Level as an additional requirement when applicable.
- Let students choose whether to evolve an eligible Pokémon or keep its current form.
- Present companion availability and progression clearly within the Companions module.
- Prevent missed study days from reducing existing progress or levels.
- Prevent XP gains from meaningless or repetitive actions.

---

## Success Criteria
- Study XP is awarded for the specified academic activities.
- Study XP increases the student’s overall Study Level.
- Study Level unlocks Pokémon in the Companions module.
- Companion Level increases only through studying with the Pokémon selected as the active companion.
- Evolution eligibility is based primarily on Companion Level and may also require Study Level.
- Eligible Pokémon display an evolution-ready state without being evolved automatically.
- The Companions module displays all available Pokémon, separates unlocked and locked Pokémon, shows required Study Levels for locked Pokémon, identifies the selected companion, and displays Companion Level and study time together.
- Missing a day does not decrease Study XP, Study Level, Companion Level, or unlocked content.
- Opening pages, clicking Pokémon, or repeatedly creating and deleting tasks does not award Study XP.

---

## Constraints
- Keep the progression system simple and motivating.
- Tie progression to real academic activity rather than passive or meaningless interactions.
- Do not force evolution when a Pokémon becomes eligible.
- Keep the progression system secondary to the actual study tools.

---

## Priority Level
- PokeDen’s actual study tools take priority; Pokémon progression should support studying without becoming the primary experience.

---

## Out of Scope
- Awarding XP for opening pages, clicking Pokémon, or repeatedly creating and deleting tasks.

---

## Context & Dependencies
- The system is being created for PokeDen.
- Pokémon unlocking and companion management belong in a dedicated Companions module.
- Progression depends on existing academic activities such as Pomodoro sessions, planned study sessions, assignments, exam-topic reviews, and daily study goals.

---

## Stakeholders
- Students using PokeDen to study and progress their Pokémon companions.

---

## Supporting Tasks

### Academic Progression
- Award Study XP when students complete eligible academic activities.
- Increase the student’s Study Level as Study XP accumulates.
- Use Study Level to unlock Pokémon.

### Companion Progression and Evolution
- Track a separate Companion Level for every Pokémon.
- Increase a Pokémon’s Companion Level only when the student studies with that Pokémon as the active companion.
- Use Companion Level as the main basis for evolution eligibility.
- Apply Study Level as an additional evolution requirement where applicable.
- Allow students to evolve eligible Pokémon or retain their current form.

### Companions Module
- Display all available Pokémon.
- Separate unlocked and locked Pokémon clearly.
- Show the required Study Level for each locked Pokémon.
- Identify the student’s currently selected companion.
- Display Companion Level and study time together.
- Indicate when a Pokémon is ready to evolve.

### Progress Integrity
- Preserve all earned progress when a student misses a day.
- Exclude non-academic and easily repeatable actions from XP calculation.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Study XP and Study Level
Study XP should come only from completing meaningful academic activities: Pomodoro sessions, planned study sessions, assignments, exam-topic reviews, and daily study goals. Accumulated Study XP should increase the student’s overall Study Level, which determines access to additional Pokémon.

### Companion Level
Each Pokémon should maintain its own Companion Level independently of the student’s Study Level. This level should increase only when the student completes study activity while that Pokémon is selected as the active companion.

### Optional Evolution
Companion Level should provide the primary progression toward evolution, while Study Level may serve as an additional requirement. When a Pokémon meets the applicable requirements, the system should indicate that evolution is ready and let the student decide whether to evolve it or keep its current form.

### Companions Module Presentation
The Companions module should provide a complete view of available Pokémon, with unlocked and locked Pokémon clearly separated. Locked Pokémon should display their required Study Level. The module should also identify the current companion, present Companion Level alongside study time, and visibly communicate evolution readiness.

### Progression Integrity
Progress should be cumulative and non-decreasing. Missing a study day must not remove progress, and actions that do not represent meaningful studying must not generate Study XP.
