# Objective
## Streamline the Student Study Planner Onboarding and Portal

---

## Role

You are a UX-focused application developer responsible for simplifying the study planner management system and maintaining a consistent user flow across the entire student portal.

---

## Description

Simplify the study planner management system by removing form fields and onboarding questions that are not necessary for students. Prioritize a straightforward, user-friendly experience over functionality that is not actively used. Update all affected dashboard and portal pages so they remain consistent with the streamlined onboarding flow. Review every page in the system for dependencies on the removed information, and align the demo data with the revised experience.

---

## Primary Objective

Create a simpler and more user-friendly student experience by removing unnecessary onboarding inputs and applying the resulting changes consistently throughout the entire system.

---

## Secondary Objectives

- Remove the year-level question and related progress or skip messaging from onboarding.
- Remove subject-code and instructor-name inputs.
- Identify and update every dashboard or portal page affected by the removed information.
- Update the demo data to match the streamlined onboarding and application flow.
- Use parallel sub-agents throughout planning and implementation.

---

## Success Criteria

- The onboarding screen no longer displays the “What year are you in?” question.
- The “Question 3 of 4 — skip any question you’d rather answer later” text is removed.
- Subject-code and instructor-name fields are removed from the relevant forms and workflows.
- The onboarding process provides a more direct student experience.
- Every page in the system is reviewed to determine whether it depends on the removed fields.
- All affected dashboard and portal pages are updated consistently.
- Demo data matches the revised onboarding and user-portal flow.
- Parallel sub-agents are used during planning and implementation.

---

## Constraints

- Prioritize usability and simplicity rather than retaining unused functionality.
- Explore every page in the system and verify whether the onboarding changes affect it.
- Use parallel sub-agents for exploration during the planning phase.
- Use multiple sub-agents simultaneously during implementation.
- Assign implementation sub-agents to different files to prevent editing conflicts.

---

## Context & Dependencies

- The system is a study planner management platform for students.
- The current onboarding flow collects the student’s year level, subject codes, and instructor names.
- Dashboard and portal functionality may currently display or depend on information collected during onboarding.
- Existing demo data may include fields or workflows that will no longer match the simplified experience.

---

## Supporting Tasks

### System Exploration
- [Tag: Parallel] Inspect the onboarding implementation and identify all components, validation rules, data models, and persistence logic related to year level, subject codes, and instructor names.
- [Tag: Parallel] Explore every dashboard and student-portal page to identify displays, forms, filters, or workflows that depend on the information being removed.
- [Tag: Parallel] Review the demo-data definitions and locate all records containing or requiring the removed fields.
- Consolidate the exploration findings into a coordinated implementation plan organized by file to avoid overlapping edits.

### Onboarding Simplification
- Remove the “What year are you in?” onboarding question.
- Remove the “Question 3 of 4 — skip any question you’d rather answer later” messaging.
- Remove subject-code and instructor-name inputs from the relevant onboarding or study-planning forms.
- Adjust the onboarding flow so it remains coherent and straightforward after these removals.

### Portal-Wide Updates
- [Tag: Parallel] Update affected dashboard and portal files through separate implementation sub-agents.
- Remove or revise UI elements and workflows that depend on year level, subject codes, or instructor names.
- Ensure unaffected pages continue to operate consistently with the revised onboarding flow.
- Verify every system page rather than limiting validation to the onboarding screen.

### Demo-Data Alignment
- Update demo data to remove dependencies on the eliminated fields.
- Ensure the revised demo data follows the same streamlined flow as an actual student user.

### Validation
- Verify the complete onboarding flow after removing the unnecessary questions and fields.
- Review every dashboard and portal page for visual, behavioral, and data-related effects.
- Confirm that removed information is no longer required by affected forms or workflows.
- Confirm that demo data displays correctly throughout the revised system.

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### Removed Onboarding Content

Eliminate the year-level question and its associated “Question 3 of 4 — skip any question you’d rather answer later” text. Remove subject-code and instructor-name inputs so students can complete onboarding through a more direct and focused process.

### Dashboard and Portal Impact

Treat the onboarding changes as system-wide data-flow changes rather than isolated interface edits. Inspect every page and update any component or workflow that displays, requires, or relies on the removed student, subject, or instructor information.

### Parallel Implementation Coordination

Use parallel sub-agents to explore separate areas of the system during planning. During implementation, assign multiple sub-agents to distinct files and run their work simultaneously, ensuring that their editing responsibilities do not overlap.

### Demo-Data Consistency

Revise the demo data so it accurately represents the streamlined onboarding process and remains compatible with all updated dashboard and portal pages.
